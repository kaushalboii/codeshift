Privacy Policy

Zero Knowledge

CodeShift is engineered with privacy as an absolute guarantee.

Real-time collaborative editing should be frictionless, anonymous, and unmonitored.

1. No Accounts & Zero Logins
We never require or collect your name, email address, password, or profile information. Sessions are pseudonymized and completely frictionless.
2. Zero Tracking & No Analytics
We do not use Google Analytics, tracking pixels, persistent advertising cookies, or browser fingerprinting. Your browsing activity is never monitored.
3. Client-Side Encryption & Compression
All code editor payloads are compressed using LZW and encrypted on your device before synchronization. The cloud database only stores ciphertext.
4. Private Room Access
Rooms are accessible exclusively through your unique room URL. Only individuals with whom you choose to share your room link can view or collaborate.
5. 100% Free & Open Source (MIT)
CodeShift's full source code is public and auditable on GitHub under the MIT License. Anyone can review security implementations or host a private instance.
